Before making project repository changes or creating a project checkout, read and follow the Git section of `~/.omp/agent/eci-binding-contract.md`.

Forecast leaves (every role): a leaf is a work-tree leaf under the Work estimates section of `~/.omp/agent/eci-binding-contract.md`. Its key is the forecast record ID, or a short outcome name outside ECI, plus its tree ID, such as `receiver-clock/1.2`. Its planned range is its initial estimate. Name each delegated leaf's key and planned range in its assignment. Read `date -u +%FT%TZ` at a leaf's start, just before its first action, and at its close, just after its last action and before reporting. Close includes any commit and the waits the leaf needed; a wait that is its own leaf is timed as that leaf instead. The executing agent reads; for a leaf split across parallel agents, the delegating owner reads. Unless you answer the user, send your owner a leaf-completion report at once for each leaf, even when several close in one run: key, work, planned, start or why it is missing, and close. Relay each report you receive at once. Send these reports mid-run when needed, Main included; rules against progress or completion messages do not cover them.

Parking (every role): when nothing is actionable, end the turn with no tool call and no status recap; completions of your runs and `write agent://<id>` revive you. Exception: as a subagent, while any of your own jobs (bash, background, or subagents you spawned) whose result you need still runs, park by repeating `wait` instead of ending the turn, however long it takes; omp disposes a subagent left idle past `task.agentIdleTtlMs`, which cancels its running jobs and subagents. As the main agent, `wait` only on such jobs that should finish within about an hour. Never `wait`, sleep, or probe (ps, tail, re-read) for other agents or replies: park. Before parking on anything that will not revive you itself (a lock, device, or another agent's process), tell the agent that will see it clear exactly what to watch and to `write agent://<your-id>` when it does, or run the check as your own async job. Under an active goal, before parking block every pending or in_progress todo with what it waits on (none may remain) and keep at least one blocked todo naming what you await, creating one if none exists, since continuation stops only then; on revival, unblock todos whose blocker cleared. Report only changed state; pure status needs no reply, but act on any decision or request in a report. Send a parked owner no status pings or re-requests; still deliver new requirements, corrections, decisions, index lookups, cancellations, and leaf-completion reports. Silence is non-terminal: do not retry or infer a crash.

Processes (every role): before finishing, stop every process your Bash calls started (`&`, nohup, setsid, build daemons) unless the user or your parent agent needs it kept running. Leave shared on-demand servers (adb, gpg-agent, ssh ControlPersist masters) running; they need no report. Run long-lived work as an omp named service or async job. Start build daemons with a short idle timeout (e.g. `bazel --max_idle_secs=300`). Never kill processes you did not start. Report each other kept process (PID, purpose) and each suspected orphan to the user or your parent agent.
